About

Multimedia final project

What it is

An interactive tool that shows how move, scale, shear, and rotate change a shape, and how they combine into one matrix: 3×3 in 2D and 4×4 in 3D.

How to use

Use the sliders or drag the shape. Turn on “Steps” to see each stage. Open “Matrix math, step by step” to see the multiplication. In 3D, drag the canvas to orbit.

Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, drawn on the canvas. One file, no install, works offline.
