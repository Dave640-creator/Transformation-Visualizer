        About TransformLab

        Version 1.0. An interactive way to see how geometric transformations work.

        What is TransformLab?

        TransformLab is a learning tool that shows how moving, scaling, and rotating a shape really works. It also covers reflecting and shearing. You can try every change in 2D and in 3D, and the numbers behind each change are always on screen, so you can see the math and the picture at the same time.

        What you can do here

        2D Lab

        Pick or draw a shape, then move, rotate, scale, flip, or slant it. Corners are labeled, and you can zoom and pan the plane.

        3D Lab

        Your 2D shape becomes a solid (a square becomes a cube). Whatever you do in 2D carries over, and you can add 3D moves on top.

        Transformation Matrix

        See the matrix for every step and the final matrix that is multiplied with each corner.

        Compare

        Build a list of steps and see why the order of steps changes the result.

        Learning Center

        Ten short lessons from easy to medium, each with a live demo you can try right inside the lesson.

        History

        Save a setup and load it again later. Your lessons and settings are remembered on this device.

        How it works

        Every change is turned into a matrix, a small grid of numbers. TransformLab multiplies the matrices into one final matrix and applies it to every corner of the shape. That is why the picture and the table always agree.

        1. Input

        The shape and the values you set with sliders or number boxes.

        2. Process

        Build a 3×3 matrix in 2D or a 4×4 matrix in 3D, then multiply.

        3. Output

        The new shape, the new coordinates, and the matrix.

        The order used in the labs: scale, shear, reflect, rotate, then move.

        The five transformations

        NameWhat it doesFormulaExample
        TranslationSlides every point the same distance(x + a, y + b)(1, 1) moved by (4, 2) becomes (5, 3)
        ScalingMakes a shape bigger or smaller(sx · x, sy · y)(3, 2) scaled by 2 becomes (6, 4)
        RotationTurns a shape around a pivot(x cosθ − y sinθ, x sinθ + y cosθ)(2, 0) turned 90° becomes (0, 2)
        ReflectionFlips a shape like a mirroracross the y axis: (−x, y)(3, 5) becomes (−3, 5)
        ShearingSlants a shape sideways(x + k · y, y)(1, 1) with k = 2 becomes (3, 1)

        How to read the screen

        Colors
        • Teal dashed outlineoriginal shape
        • Orange shapethe result
        • Cyan dotpivot point

        Handy tips
        • Pinch or scroll to zoom the 2D plane.
        • Tap Draw shape, tap points, then Finish. TransformLab names your shape.
        • Tap See it in 3D to turn it into a solid.
        • Letters mark corners, and the table shows their numbers.
